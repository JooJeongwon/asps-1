# TEAM 04 배경

OpenAI 내장 `image_gen`으로 생성한 프로젝트 룸·학교·라운지 배경 시트다. 코드나 Python으로 이미지를 그리거나 편집하지 않았다.

저장 파일: [`public/assets/team04-scenes.png`](../public/assets/team04-scenes.png)

위 왼쪽은 아침 학교 외부, 위 오른쪽은 낮 프로젝트 룸, 아래 왼쪽은 복도와 라운지, 아래 오른쪽은 밤 프로젝트 룸이다. 시나리오의 `scene: 0, 1, 2, 3`에 대응한다.

최종 생성 프롬프트:

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Create a single original BACKGROUND SPRITE SHEET for a Korean university team-project romance visual novel. One WIDE LANDSCAPE canvas, 16:9 ratio, divided exactly into a 2 by 2 grid of four equal rectangular background panels, no gaps, borders or labels. Each panel is a complete wide scenic background. NO PEOPLE, NO CHARACTERS, no silhouettes.
Top left: exterior of a modern Korean university building on a September morning, glass entry doors, broad steps, a few green trees and warm clear sunlight.
Top right: inviting student project room in daytime, big meeting table, four chairs, open laptops, a whiteboard with indistinct diagram shapes and no readable text, tall windows, warm wood and cream details.
Bottom left: cozy campus lounge next to a bright corridor, window seats, small cafe tables and a vending machine at one side, late afternoon sunlight, drinks with no branding.
Bottom right: the SAME project room from top right but at night, warm task lamps and cool city lights through dark windows, laptops glowing, a coffee cup and sticky notes, intimate and calm feeling after teamwork.
Consistent premium 2D anime background art, fine illustrated architectural details, painterly cel shading, muted warm ivory, lilac and soft gold palette matching an elegant Korean anime romance game. Foreground centers open for character portraits. No real photos, no legible text, no UI, no logos, no watermarks.
```
